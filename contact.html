FAQ

Questions Before You Contact Us?

Here are some common questions our clients ask.

01
Do you offer custom fabrication?
+
Yes, we specialize in custom engineering and fabrication tailored precisely to client drawings, technical specifications, and specific operational standards.
02
What drawings or specifications do you need?
+
We accept 2D CAD drawings (DWG/PDF), 3D models, or process datasheets detailing design pressure, temperature rating, and material preferences (SS304, SS316, Carbon Steel, etc.).
03
How long does it take to get a quotation?
+
Our estimating team typically provides complete commercial and technical proposals within 24 to 48 hours of receiving complete specifications.
04
What is the typical production timeline?
+
Timelines depend on project complexity, raw material availability, and volume. Standard items take 2 to 4 weeks, while complex process skids take 4 to 8 weeks.
05
Can I visit your facility before placing an order?
+
Absolutely! We welcome client visits, technical audits, and pre-inspection walkthroughs at our manufacturing facility in Vadodara, Gujarat.
